Books

Ledgers, not markets.

The control spreadsheet is organized into books — named places for traces. These are labels for stewardship, not product tickers.

01Fiat rails

Bank, card, payroll, and cash movement that funds mission work. Note origin, purpose, and steward.

02Crypto rails

On-chain and exchange-adjacent traces when used for mission continuity. Wallet identity is private; the purpose line is public enough to be honest.

03Mission ops

Hosting, tools, legal, land, and household ops that keep One Mission running. Split vanity spend from continuity spend.

04Gifts and support

Inbound gifts and outbound support. Never reframe a gift as investment performance.

See Rules for how entries are written.
