Rules

How a signal earns its line.

Simple rules so the trust loop stays usable under stress — and does not become a casino.

01Feedback is gathered to find consilience — many witnesses, one coherent truth — not to replace the Creator’s steward with a crowd.

02The 13 Shared Values sieve what may stay. A popular signal that fails the values is not entered.

03Purpose is written in plain language a stranger could audit without a private myth.

04Every money trace has a date, a book, an amount (or range), a purpose, a lane, and a steward initials line. Amount labels are language, not balances.

05Personal, charity Dreams, and Intek lanes do not mix. Co-mingling requires a named transfer note.

06Crypto traces record chain/rail when known; they never invent a price prediction. Closed means reconciled to a receipt, statement, or on-chain confirmation — not hope.

07Published live traces are HITL (or soft platform observe). Soft $ is not an invoice. Gift rail never merges with PAYG. X Money and tip-dreams badges are labeled traces only.

08If a line would violate Refused, it is not entered — rewrite the action instead. Foundation self-stewardship is not replaced by collective noise.
